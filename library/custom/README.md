# Own fixture definitions

Fixtures that are not (yet) part of the QLC+ library, in the QLC+ `.qxf` format.
They are embedded into the plugin next to the QLC+ library and appear under their
manufacturer in the *Fixture Library* tab. Licensed under the project's license (GPL-3.0-or-later);
they may also be contributed to QLC+.

| File | Notes |
|---|---|
| `Generic-Wall-Washer-9x15W-RGBW.qxf` | 9 × 15 W RGBW wall washer, 4/8-channel mode (also sold under UKing and other names) |
| `UKing-Wall-Washer-9x15W-RGBW.qxf` | same device, listed under UKing |
